## Your data

The software sends nothing anywhere. Recordings, spreadsheets and run reports stay in
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\NtamarasGeneral` on your machine.

The single exception is the optional vision feature, which is **off by default**. If you
switch it on and supply your own Anthropic API key, a screenshot of the failing window is
sent to Anthropic to identify a control that could not otherwise be found. It is your key,
your account and your choice.

The optional update check, also off by default, requests a small JSON file and sends
nothing about you.
